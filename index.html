What it does

This is a personal integration built around Google API tools exposed through the Model Context Protocol (MCP). The tools available depend on the MCP servers and permissions you enable.

Google Drive

Find and organize files

Search your authorized files, retrieve content or metadata, upload or export documents, and manage folders, sharing and file organization when you request those actions.

Google Slides

Read and edit presentations

Read slides and speaker notes, create presentations, edit text and visual elements, arrange slides, and export presentations using the enabled tools.

Why it asks
for access

The configured Drive and Slides servers request the following permissions. The Google consent screen shows the permissions for each connection; access is limited by the account and permissions you grant.

Google Drivehttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive

Allows reading and managing accessible Drive files, including existing files that you ask the assistant to find or edit, exports, comments and sharing. This is broad access: enabled tools may also move files to trash or delete them. Per-file access would limit discovery and management of existing files across your Drive.

Google Slideshttps://www.googleapis.com/auth/presentations

Allows reading and editing presentations, including slide content, shapes, tables and speaker notes. The Slides connection also uses Drive access for file operations such as export and sharing.

Account identityopenid
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email

Identifies the Google account linked to the integration, so the connection can show which account is authorized.

Your instructions determine the task. Review actions in your assistant and configure its confirmation settings for changes to files and sharing. The breadth of an OAuth permission is separate from the specific task you request.

Where your
data goes

OAuth credentials are saved by the configured MCP server on the machine running it. Tool results can enter the assistant conversation and be sent to the AI provider you choose to fulfill your request. Your client and provider settings govern conversation storage and processing.

This public website provides information only. It has no Google sign-in, file upload, analytics scripts or access to your Google files. Read the privacy policy for details.

Connect and
stay in control

  1. Configure the Drive or Slides MCP server in your assistant client.
  2. Use the server’s login action to open Google’s authorization page. Review the app identity and requested permissions before approving.
  3. Choose a file or presentation and ask the assistant to perform a task. Check the result and any requested confirmations.

You can withdraw Google access at any time in your Google Account connections.
